# RABA Client Acceptance and Capacity Gate v0.1

**Status:** Accepted operational control material
**Canonical status:** Non-canonical
**Authority:** Human Owner
**Purpose:** Provide a mandatory pre-engagement and ongoing capacity gate for RABA consulting work
**Version:** v0.1
**Date:** 2026-08-04

> This gate supports disciplined client acceptance.
> Passing the gate does not establish compliance, validation, safety, readiness, endorsement, or Canon.
> Final client acceptance remains a Human Owner decision.

---

## 1. Gate rule

No paid or unpaid consulting engagement should begin until the following have been assessed:

- Client Fit;
- Claim Fit;
- Method Fit;
- Capacity Fit;
- Authority Fit;
- Conflict-of-Interest Fit;
- Data-Handling Readiness;
- Delivery Boundary;
- Research Reserve impact;
- Stop conditions.

The gate result must be explicit.

---

## 2. Allowed outcomes

```text
accept
accept with conditions
rescope
waitlist
refer out
decline
```

No AI recommendation or lead score may automatically produce `accept`.

---

## 3. Client Fit

Check:

- Is the client facing a real AI-enabled governance, responsibility, authority, oversight, or traceability problem?
- Is the requested work within RABA's present competence?
- Is the problem sufficiently bounded?
- Is the client seeking analysis and improvement rather than a predetermined endorsement?
- Is the client an appropriate fit for the current maturity of RABA consulting?

Possible failure indicators:

- request is primarily legal advice;
- request is primarily certification or formal audit;
- request requires specialised technical assurance beyond current competence;
- request is too broad to assess responsibly;
- client wants promotional endorsement rather than analysis.

---

## 4. Claim Fit

Check:

- What result does the client expect?
- Can that result be described without unsupported compliance, validation, certification, assurance, adoption, or readiness claims?
- Are non-claims visible before engagement?
- Is the client willing to accept uncertainty and limitations?
- Could the deliverable be misused as approval or certification?

Required response where claim fit fails:

```text
rescope / refer out / decline
```

---

## 5. Method Fit

Check:

- Can the engagement follow the RABA consulting workflow?
- Can material evidence be reviewed?
- Can responsibility and authority boundaries be reconstructed?
- Can uncertainty and evidence gaps be recorded?
- Can Human Owner review occur before delivery?
- Can the scope be completed without removing essential controls?

If the deadline requires removal of essential controls, reduce the scope or decline.

---

## 6. Capacity Fit

Check:

- How many active pilots or clients currently exist?
- Is the initial maximum of two active pilots already reached?
- Is the work compatible with protected family time?
- Is the work compatible with employment obligations?
- Is sufficient time available for review and documentation?
- Will accepting the work displace the research reserve?
- Is there realistic capacity for revisions and follow-up?

Initial capacity defaults:

```text
maximum active pilots: 2
maximum consulting delivery days per week: 2
minimum protected research block per week: 1
minimum weekly governance review: 1
```

Any exception requires a separate Human Owner capacity decision.

---

## 7. Authority Fit

Check:

- Is the person requesting the work authorized to define scope?
- Is the person able to provide evidence and access?
- Is the person authorized to receive the deliverable?
- Who may approve scope changes?
- Who may authorize public use?
- Who may authorize payment?
- Who is responsible for operational decisions after the engagement?

Commercial contact does not automatically equal decision authority.

---

## 8. Conflict-of-Interest Fit

Check:

- Is payment dependent on a positive conclusion?
- Is the client asking RABA to validate a prior decision?
- Is there pressure to omit or soften findings?
- Is there exclusivity pressure?
- Would one client become commercially dominant?
- Is there a relationship or partnership expectation that may affect independence?
- Is RABA being asked to endorse a product, provider, or individual?

Any material conflict must be recorded and resolved before acceptance.

---

## 9. Data-Handling Readiness

Check:

- What client information will be processed?
- Does it include personal, confidential, regulated, proprietary, health, employment, financial, or security-sensitive information?
- Which AI systems or tools may receive the information?
- Has the client been informed where appropriate?
- Can unnecessary identifying details be removed?
- Is storage location known?
- Is deletion or retention responsibility defined?
- Can the work proceed with synthetic, anonymized, or minimized data?

If data cannot be handled responsibly, pause, rescope, or decline.

---

## 10. Delivery Boundary

Before acceptance, define:

- one bounded problem or workflow;
- purpose of the engagement;
- included evidence;
- excluded areas;
- deliverables;
- duration;
- price;
- revision limit;
- follow-up;
- non-claims;
- blocked conclusions;
- client responsibilities;
- RABA responsibilities;
- stop conditions.

Scope expansion requires:

```text
new scope review
→ new capacity review
→ revised price or timing
→ Human Owner decision
```

---

## 11. Research Reserve Impact

Check:

- Does the engagement preserve protected research and governance time?
- Does it create a valuable research question?
- Is any cross-case learning permitted and appropriately anonymized?
- Is client-specific learning being kept separate from RABA Canon?
- Will commercial urgency displace existing governance obligations?

Client delivery must not silently consume the research reserve.

---

## 12. Commercial Integrity Check

Confirm alignment with:

```text
RABA Commercial Non-Negotiables v0.1
```

Required confirmations:

- no predetermined conclusion;
- no unsupported claim;
- no hidden waiver;
- no client-driven canonicalization;
- no suppression of material uncertainty;
- no authority drift;
- no public-positioning commitment;
- no partnership implication without approval.

## 14. Ongoing capacity gate

Re-run the gate when:

- scope expands;
- deadline changes materially;
- new data categories appear;
- public use is requested;
- client requests stronger claims;
- payment becomes conditional on outcome;
- a partnership or referral arrangement emerges;
- workload crosses the capacity limit;
- family, health, or employment conditions change;
- material conflict or uncertainty appears.

---

## 15. Stop conditions after acceptance

Pause the engagement when:

- material evidence is unavailable;
- requested claims exceed the evidence;
- scope changes without approval;
- client authority is unclear;
- confidentiality or data handling becomes unsafe;
- workload becomes unsustainable;
- review quality cannot be maintained;
- the client seeks suppression of findings;
- the engagement is being presented as compliance, validation, certification, or endorsement;
- commercial pressure threatens the Non-Negotiables.

Resume only after the issue is explicitly resolved.

---

## 16. Completion boundary

Completion of an engagement means only that the agreed bounded deliverables were provided.

It does not establish:

- client compliance;
- safe deployment;
- risk elimination;
- formal assurance;
- certification;
- RABA validation;
- RABA Canon;
- public endorsement.

---

## 17. Final authority

AI systems may:

- extract facts;
- identify risks;
- score fit;
- compare capacity;
- prepare gate records;
- recommend outcomes.

AI systems must not:

- accept a client;
- waive a condition;
- approve scope expansion;
- authorize use of client data;
- approve public claims;
- approve partnership;
- approve exceptions;
- make final delivery decisions.

Final client acceptance and capacity authority remain with the Human Owner.
