# RABA Commercial Non-Negotiables v0.1

**Status:** Accepted operational control material
**Canonical status:** Non-canonical
**Authority:** Human Owner
**Purpose:** Protect RABA integrity from commercial pressure, revenue incentives, client preference, deadline pressure, partnership opportunity, and market attention
**Version:** v0.1
**Date:** 2026-08-04

> This document governs internal commercial conduct and consulting boundaries.
> It does not establish RABA Canon, validation, compliance, certification, assurance, adoption, endorsement, or market readiness.
> Final authority remains with the Human Owner.

---

## 1. Core rule

```text
Revenue is evidence of demand.
Revenue is not evidence of correctness.
Client satisfaction is not validation.
Commercial success is not Canon.
```

Commercial activity may support RABA development, but it must not silently acquire architectural, governance, canonical, or public-positioning authority.

---

## 2. What cannot be sold

The following may not be waived, weakened, concealed, or exchanged for revenue, deadlines, client preference, partnership opportunity, reputation, access, or market visibility:

- Human Owner authority;
- explicit distinction between AI assistance and Human Owner decision;
- material uncertainty;
- material disagreement;
- evidence gaps;
- responsibility and authority boundaries;
- traceability of consequential decisions;
- separation of review input from approval;
- separation of repository presence, commit, push, PR, merge, and Canon;
- separation of client feedback from architectural authority;
- truthful non-claims;
- right to decline, rescope, pause, or stop work;
- responsible handling of client information;
- independent professional judgment within the stated scope.

---

## 3. Claim boundary

RABA consulting must not claim, imply, or allow clients to represent that an engagement establishes:

- legal compliance;
- EU AI Act compliance;
- regulatory approval;
- certification;
- assurance;
- audit completion;
- formal validation;
- production readiness;
- safety guarantee;
- risk elimination;
- adoption or endorsement;
- architectural Canon;
- market acceptance;
- provider authorization;
- partnership status;
- roadmap approval.

Any stronger claim requires separate evidence, appropriate qualified professionals, and explicit Human Owner approval.

---

## 4. Independence of conclusions

Payment does not entitle a client to:

- a positive conclusion;
- removal of material findings;
- suppression of uncertainty;
- preferred wording that misstates evidence;
- confirmation of a preselected outcome;
- endorsement of deployment;
- endorsement of a product, provider, partner, or organisation;
- canonical influence over RABA.

If a client requests a predetermined conclusion, the engagement must be declined, rescoped, or stopped.

---

## 5. Client feedback boundary

Client feedback, repeated requests, purchasing behaviour, and market demand are:

```text
external signal / commercial signal / review input
```

They are not:

```text
validation / architectural approval / Human Owner decision / Canon
```

Commercial demand may create:

- a research question;
- a pattern candidate;
- an operational improvement candidate;
- a canonicalization candidate.

It must not directly modify RABA architecture or Canon.

---

## 6. Commercial pressure boundary

The following must be treated as governance-relevant pressure signals:

- fear of losing a client;
- dependence on one client;
- urgency imposed by a deadline;
- pressure to simplify or omit review;
- pressure to overstate certainty;
- pressure to use compliance or certification language;
- pressure to conceal a material limitation;
- pressure to treat an AI-generated result as approved;
- pressure to present a pilot as validation;
- pressure to convert a client-specific exception into a general rule;
- pressure to let commercial priorities displace protected research and governance work.

Such signals must be surfaced, not normalized.

---

## 7. Commercial concentration boundary

No single client should become so commercially important that RABA cannot safely:

- disagree;
- disclose a material limitation;
- refuse an unsupported claim;
- pause work;
- decline additional scope;
- end the relationship.

A sustained dependency approaching or exceeding approximately one third of RABA consulting income from one client should trigger explicit Human Owner review.

This threshold is a governance trigger, not an automatic prohibition.

---

## 8. Research reserve

Commercial delivery must not consume all available RABA capacity.

During the initial commercial trial, the working allocation should preserve:

- up to 60% of available RABA time for client delivery and market discovery;
- at least 25% for research, method development, and cross-case learning;
- at least 15% for governance, documentation, review, and traceability.

These percentages are operational defaults and may be changed only by an explicit Human Owner capacity decision.

---

## 9. Protected review requirements

Commercial urgency must not silently remove:

- intake review;
- scope definition;
- evidence-status review;
- uncertainty assessment;
- responsibility-boundary analysis;
- claim review;
- Human Owner decision points;
- final deliverable review;
- blocked-actions record.

Where time or evidence is insufficient, the scope must be reduced rather than the controls removed.

---

## 10. Stop triggers

### 10.1 Integrity stop

Pause or stop when the work requires:

- an unsupported claim;
- concealment of material uncertainty;
- removal of a material finding;
- misrepresentation of approval or authority;
- compliance, certification, validation, or assurance wording without adequate basis.

### 10.2 Capacity stop

Pause or stop when delivery threatens:

- family sustainability;
- health;
- essential employment obligations;
- quality of client work;
- protected research time;
- governance and review capacity.

### 10.3 Architecture stop

Pause or stop when repeated client-specific exceptions begin to alter RABA method, principles, or public positioning without a separate research and Human Owner decision process.

### 10.4 Data-handling stop

Pause or stop when required client data cannot be handled responsibly under the current tools, permissions, agreements, or confidentiality controls.

---

## 11. Right to decline or rescope

RABA retains the right to:

- decline;
- rescope;
- postpone;
- waitlist;
- refer out;
- terminate an engagement.

Revenue opportunity does not override this right.

---

## 12. Public-positioning boundary

No client engagement may be publicly described as:

- validation of RABA;
- proof of compliance;
- certification;
- market adoption;
- institutional endorsement;
- partnership;
- proven methodology;
- successful implementation;

without separate evidence, client permission where required, and explicit Human Owner approval.

An anonymized learning note remains review material unless separately approved for publication.

---

## 13. Weekly commercial integrity review

During active commercial work, RABA should review:

- what commercial pressure influenced decisions;
- what was accelerated or omitted;
- whether claims became stronger;
- whether uncertainty became less visible;
- whether protected research was displaced;
- whether client-specific exceptions appeared;
- whether client feedback is being mistaken for architectural authority;
- whether workload remains sustainable;
- whether any stop trigger has been crossed.

Output status:

```text
weekly commercial integrity note /
operational control material /
non-canonical
```

---

## 14. Final authority

AI systems may identify pressure, risk, drift, evidence gaps, and possible controls.

AI systems must not:

- waive these Non-Negotiables;
- accept a client;
- approve a claim;
- authorize public use;
- authorize partnership;
- authorize commercial exceptions;
- adopt architecture;
- canonicalize material.

Final authority remains with the Human Owner.
